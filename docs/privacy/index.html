Questbound Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Questbound is a free, unofficial game-guide application published by SliverzFX. This policy explains how the application handles information.

Information handled by the app

Questbound does not require an account, and its guide content and game tools are designed to work offline. Based on the current version of the application, we do not automatically collect or transmit personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports to our servers. We do not run advertisements or tracking analytics in the app.

Saved preferences and progress

Questbound stores information such as quest progress, favorites, and settings locally on your device so its features work. You can choose to export a backup and determine where to save or share that file. Backups are not automatically uploaded by Questbound. If you share or store a backup using another service, that service may process it according to its own privacy practices.

Optional bug reports and quest corrections

The Support screen can prepare a report containing the app version, device manufacturer and model, Android version, and, when applicable, the selected game, quest title, and quest identifier. The report is copied to your clipboard only when you press the copy button. Questbound does not send the report automatically. You decide whether to paste and share it with us using Discord or another method. Information you voluntarily share may be visible to the recipients or members of the channel where you post it. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in reports.

External services and donations

Questbound provides optional links to external websites, including Discord for support and Wise and Ko-fi for voluntary donations. These websites are operated by third parties and may collect information when you visit them or make payments. Their privacy policies apply to your activity there. Questbound does not collect or process your payment details. Donations do not unlock app features.

Children's privacy

Questbound is not designed to collect children's personal information. Users should avoid sharing personal or sensitive details through external support services.

Data control

You can remove locally stored Questbound information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, subject to your device's backup settings. Files you deliberately exported must be deleted separately wherever you saved them. For information you shared on third-party services, refer to those services' controls and policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact sliverz.sfx@gmail.com.

Questbound is an unofficial fan-made guide and is not affiliated with the publishers or developers of the games it covers.
